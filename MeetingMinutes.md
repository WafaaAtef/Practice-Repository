# OSS Mentorship

## What is Open Source?

- Free public software.
- A large codebase.
- You can contribute by solving an issue or building a new feature, and then make a **Pull Request (PR)**.

## GSoC (Google Summer of Code)

- From March until the beginning of April.
- A lot of companies produce many projects that need contributors.
- Each project has a small, abstract description. If you have any questions, it will be a good point to ask about them.
- Some projects have a specific duration to finish, while others may continue for many releases.
- You can contribute to small issues at first. If your contributions are done well, you could be hired.
- You could be accepted into a project and then find that you need some prerequisites.
- Using **Linux OS** will be good, as many dependencies are easier to install and work with than on Windows.

## OLFX

-opens three times.
- There are interviews.
- All projects are paid.
- All topics are open.
- You don't need all the project, just specific features or bugs.
- It's not just your mentor; there are meetings each week.
- Your English leveldoesn't matter it will be enhanced.
